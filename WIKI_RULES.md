# Knowledge Guild RPG Rules

- A Guild avatar is a UI representation of permitted Wiki records, never a real person, inferred personality, or professional credential.
- Each member owns an independent `members/{member-id}/` Wiki. Do not mix members' opinions or treat missing records as disagreement.
- Label facts, personal opinions, hypotheses, and unverified material. A synthesis draft is not an official decision.
- `raw/`, `output/`, private profile/context files, local app settings, and secrets stay local and are never indexed or committed.
- The Mission Board uses only `projects/` common context. Official decisions exist only in `decisions/` after explicit agreement.
- Guild activity pose may use a public repository path's last-change date only. It never states a member's real activity, intent, or availability.
